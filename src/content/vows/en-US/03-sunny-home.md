We hope our future home has a window open to the sky, and enough room for books, plants and small keepsakes from our journeys.

## Our little-home list

- A table made for sharing meals
- A corner filled with sunlight on clear days
- Plants that can grow slowly beside us
- A wall for photographs from our travels and everyday life

---

A house is only a space. The marks of a life thoughtfully shared are what make it a home.
