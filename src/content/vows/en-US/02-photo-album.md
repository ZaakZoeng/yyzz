Our album should hold more than journeys. It should also keep the ordinary days we may unexpectedly miss one day.

## Moments to collect

1. The cheerful mess of cooking together
2. A sunset found on the way home
3. Trees and streets from a weekend walk
4. One photograph from every anniversary

## A note about privacy

Before a photograph appears here, it will be pixelated or stylised and stripped of location and device metadata. The original stays only in our private album.
